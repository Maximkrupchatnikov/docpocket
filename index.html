Subscriptions and purchases

  • Cancel the yearly plan: Settings on your iPhone or iPad > your name > Subscriptions > DocPocket Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the year ends.
  • Restore Pro on a new device: open DocPocket > Settings > the plan card > Upgrade, then tap Restore at the top, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your documents

Documents and everything DocPocket learns from them stay on your device. There is no DocPocket account and no cloud copy, so we cannot see or recover your papers. They are part of your device backup (iCloud or computer) according to your backup settings, so restoring that backup restores them.
